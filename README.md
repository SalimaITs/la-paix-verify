# LA PAIX Verify

The peace you find in your security.

LA PAIX Verify helps mid-sized companies protect themselves against fraud
using fake phone calls, video meetings and emails. The idea is simple:
before anyone releases a payment, changes bank details or resets a
password, the real person confirms the request on their own device.
A fraudster can fake a voice, but not the managing director's phone.

## Why I am building this

In my work, I see how often phishing emails
are still clicked despite training. AI makes these attacks even more
convincing. Large corporations buy expensive solutions against them.
For mid-sized companies there is hardly anything that is simple enough
and fits European data protection requirements.

## What LA PAIX Verify does

- **Confirmations:** approve or reject requests in the app, with a
  rotating code for phone calls and a four-eyes check for large amounts
- **Security policy:** set the rules, and the app turns them into a
  policy aligned with ISO 27001 and NIS2
- **Training:** three short courses based on real cases, each with a quiz
- **Monthly report:** confirmations, stopped fraud attempts and training
  status compared with the previous month

## Status

Working prototype with a fictional sample company. Before it can be used
with real customers, it still needs proper user accounts, push
notifications and its own server.

- `lapaix/` – first version
- `lapaix-v2/` (branch `lapaix-v2`) – revised version with an
  introduction and simpler navigation

To try it, open `index.html` in a browser. Without a server, the app runs
with sample data that is stored only in your own browser.
